Our approach

How CHART works

CHART’s approach combines community listening, partnership design, technical expertise, implementation planning and transparent learning.

Listen with communities

Start with local priorities, existing groups and lived realities.

Design with partners

Build programmes with community-based organizations and technical collaborators.

Deliver responsibly

Use clear roles, budgets, timelines and monitoring practices.

Learn and adapt

Document learning, improve models and report clearly to stakeholders.

Governance & Accountability

Responsible stewardship and transparent systems

CHART’s governance model is designed to support accountability, clarity and responsible decision-making as the organization grows.

Board of Trustees

Governance, strategy and financial oversight that supports accountability, transparency and responsible stewardship.

Finance Committee

Budget oversight, controls and financial reporting to support responsible use of resources.

Clear authority lines

Defined roles and decision-making processes across governance, management and programme teams.

Compliance culture

Commitment to audits, transparent reporting and relevant charity governance standards.
