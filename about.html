Vision and values

About CHART

A clearer story of CHART Initiative, its purpose, values and leadership.

Our vision

Healthy, resilient and creative communities

CHART envisions communities where healthcare is accessible, livelihoods are resilient, environments are protected and people can participate in shaping their own futures.

✓
Mission

To bring together health, climate action, livelihoods and art-based approaches that respond to community priorities.

✓
Purpose

To connect technical expertise, partnerships and local knowledge into practical programmes that improve wellbeing.

✓
Operating footprint

CHART is rooted in Kenya and the United Kingdom, with partnerships that support learning across communities.

Core values

Values that guide how CHART works

The organization’s values create a practical standard for partnerships, programme design and public accountability.

Community-centered

We start with lived realities, local priorities and the leadership of community partners.

Accountable

We value transparency, good governance and responsible stewardship.

Collaborative

We work through partnerships across community groups, technical experts and institutions.

Inclusive

We support women, youth and marginalized groups to participate and lead.

Creative

We use art and storytelling as tools for dignity, healing and social change.

Sustainable

We focus on practical models that can grow responsibly over time.
