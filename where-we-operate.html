Where We Operate

CHART is connected to communities and partners in Kenya and the United Kingdom, with a focus on underserved and climate-vulnerable contexts.

Operating footprint

A clearer footprint for community-led work

CHART’s work is organized around Kenya and the United Kingdom, connecting local partnerships, governance support and community priorities across underserved and climate-vulnerable contexts.

Kenya

Nairobi, Mombasa, Kitui, Kwale, Tana River and other counties are part of CHART’s community and partner focus.

United Kingdom

UK links support mental health, arts, inclusion, partnerships and governance connections.

ASAL and underserved contexts

Climate-vulnerable and underserved communities are prioritized because integrated support can create practical value.
