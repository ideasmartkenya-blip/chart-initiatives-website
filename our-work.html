Our Work

CHART’s programme portfolio connects health, climate resilience, livelihoods and creative social change.

Programme portfolio

Five connected areas of work

Instead of treating challenges separately, CHART links programme areas so communities can access integrated support.

Maternal Health & Emergency Referral

Improving pathways for safer maternal care, timely referral and community awareness.

Mental Health & Community Wellbeing

Building awareness, support and culturally sensitive approaches to mental wellbeing.

Mobile Dental Outreach

Bringing essential oral health services closer to underserved communities.

Climate Action & Livelihoods

Supporting climate-resilient livelihoods, food security and sustainable community enterprise.

Art for Social Change

Using creativity, culture and storytelling to strengthen inclusion, healing and civic voice.

Integrated Approach

Coordinating programmes so health, environment, livelihoods and culture reinforce one another.

Why it matters

Communities need joined-up solutions

Health access, mental wellbeing, climate stress, livelihoods and social exclusion often overlap. CHART’s structure helps programmes respond to those connections with practical support.
